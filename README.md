# Offline Photo Editor

A simple photo editor that runs directly in your browser and works completely offline once installed to your device's home screen.

## Features
- Crop, rotate, flip, and horizon leveling
- Brush tool, blur brush, pixelate brush, black bars, and shapes
- Text tool (custom fonts, color, outline, shadow), layers, opacity control, filters, and background fills
- Infinite zoom, full undo and redo history
- Completely free: no ads, accounts, subscriptions, watermarks, or AI processing

## Privacy
All images are processed **on your device only**. The application never uploads your files to external servers, uses no analytics, cookies, or trackers, and collects zero personal data.
Saved images are exported directly to your device's downloads folder.

## How to Install on Mobile
1. Open the project URL in Google Chrome (or your primary browser).
2. Tap the browser menu (⋮) → **"Install app"** (or **"Add to Home screen"**).
3. Once installed, the application will launch and function fully without an internet connection.
